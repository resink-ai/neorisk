# Brief: posts and repost cascades

**Business.** About 1,500 users follow about 25 accounts each; popular accounts attract most follows, and a quarter of follows are returned. Users post about once every eight days. A follower sees a post with probability 0.35 after a few hours; 15% of viewers like it and 6% repost it to their own followers. A user sees and reposts each post at most once.

**Who acts.** Users (owners); each post's cascade (an owner created by the post).

## Rates we know

| # | Fact | Value |
|---|------|-------|
| 1 | Viewers who repost | about 6% |
| 2 | Cascades several levels deep | some |
